How to Find Your ICS Feed URL

  1. Log in to Blackbaud (myschoolapp.com) with your parent account.
  2. Navigate to your student's Calendar page.
  3. Look for a link or button labeled "Subscribe to Calendar", "iCal Feed", or "Export" — it's usually in the top-right area or under a settings/gear icon.
  4. Copy the URL it gives you. It will look something like:
    https://yourschool.myschoolapp.com/podium/feed/iCal.aspx?z=abc123...
  5. Paste that URL into the field above and click Generate Planner.

The URL acts as a private key to your child's calendar. We never store it — see our privacy policy below.

Privacy & Security

We take your family's data seriously. Here's exactly what happens:

  • Your ICS URL is never stored, logged, or retained. It is used for a single fetch to read your child's schedule, then immediately discarded.
  • Schedule data never touches a database or file system. It exists only in memory for the few seconds it takes to generate your PDF.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies. This page is static HTML.
  • The code is open source. You can verify every claim by reading the public repository.
  • API Gateway logging is explicitly disabled for request/response bodies so your URL cannot accidentally end up in cloud logs.
