How to Find Your ICS Feed URL

  1. Log in to Blackbaud (myschoolapp.com) with your parent account.
  2. Click the Children menu at the top and select your middle school student.
  3. On their profile page, click Schedule, then View Full Schedule.
  4. Click the Share button in the upper left.
  5. Click the Copy button next to the "Feed URL" textbox.
  6. Paste that URL into the field above and click Generate Planner.

The URL acts as a private key to your child's calendar. We never store it — see our privacy policy below.

Privacy & Security

We take your family's data seriously. Here's exactly what happens:

  • Your ICS URL is never stored, logged, or retained on our servers. It is used for a single fetch to read your child's schedule, then immediately discarded.
  • Schedule data never touches a database or file system. It exists only in memory for the few seconds it takes to generate your PDF.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies. This page is static HTML.
  • Optional "save in my browser" is 100% on your device. If you tick that box, your details (URL, name, grade, theme, and options) are saved in this browser's local storage — never sent to us or anyone else. They're used only to pre-fill the form next time you visit on this device. Unchecking the box, or clicking "Clear saved data," erases them immediately, and they auto-expire after 90 days if unused. Please don't use it on a shared or public computer, since the URL is a private key to your child's calendar.
  • The code is open source. You can verify every claim by reading the public repository.
  • API Gateway logging is explicitly disabled for request/response bodies so your URL cannot accidentally end up in cloud logs.
